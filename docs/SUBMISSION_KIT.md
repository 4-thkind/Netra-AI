## 📝 Project Description (Copy-Paste Text)

```text
Netrā is an AI growth copilot for Paytm merchants that transforms the collective transaction intelligence of 4.9 crore merchants into proactive, personalized growth moves for each individual merchant. It clusters merchants into hyperlocal micro-markets, detects demand gaps, tracks pricing dynamics, predicts cash flow, auto-generates festival offers, and assigns weekly growth missions learned from similar merchants who grew. Delivered through the Paytm Soundbox (voice, 11+ languages via Sarvam AI), the Paytm for Business app, and WhatsApp nudges (via n8n automation). Each merchant's preferences, patterns, and interaction history persist across sessions via a Cognee-powered knowledge graph. Netrā is not a dashboard. It is a proactive intelligence layer that comes to the merchant with moves before they know to ask.
```
