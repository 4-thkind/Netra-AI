The Intelligence Layer — Reading the Market

Trade Radar

Hyperlocal Demand Heatmap

"See what nearby consumers are buying that you do not stock."

Netrā clusters every merchant into a hyperlocal micro-market — roughly a 1 to 2 km radius zone aligned with a real neighborhood (Lajpat Nagar, not "Delhi"). Within that zone, it aggregates transaction volumes by product category across all merchants. Beverages up 40% this week. Snacks steady. Personal care down 10%. Then it compares this market-level demand heatmap against YOUR transaction categories. If iced beverages are surging in Lajpat Nagar and Ramesh has zero transactions in that category, that is a gap. That gap becomes a Trade Radar alert: "Iced tea is up 40% in your pin code. You stock none. Top 3 items to try based on what similar stores sell: [X, Y, Z]." The ML underneath is collaborative filtering on a merchant-category matrix, localized by micro-market. Lightweight. Interpretable. Runs as a daily batch job on Paytm's existing transaction data.

Price Pulse

Statistical ATV Analysis

"Know where your pricing stands in the local market."

Netrā computes the average transaction value per product category per micro-market. Across all kiranas in Lajpat Nagar, the median snack transaction is ₹35. Ramesh's average is ₹40 — he is pricing 14% above the median. Netrā tracks this gap over time: is it widening? Are nearby merchants lowering prices? Is his volume dropping as a result? When it detects a risk, it does not just say "you are expensive" — that is demoralizing and useless. It frames it as an opportunity: "Your snack pricing is 14% above the area average and your volume dipped 8% this week. A weekend combo — namkeen + cold drink at ₹30 — could pull price-sensitive customers back without a permanent price cut." No merchant ever sees another merchant's prices. Only aggregate market averages. Privacy is absolute. The model is statistical outlier detection on average-transaction-value distributions, tracked over a rolling 30-day window.

NETRĀ The Action Layer
05 / 10

The Action Layer — Moving Before the Market Does

Festival Engine

Auto-generates localized offers. One tap to send.

Netrā maintains a master calendar of Indian festivals — Diwali, Holi, Chhath Puja, Onam, Pongal, Baisakhi, and local melas. For each merchant, it pulls sales data from the same festival last year: what spiked, what sold out, what sat on shelves. Sarvam's LLM generates a complete, ready-to-send offer package: WhatsApp message in the merchant's language, inventory prep list ("you ran out of X last time — order 20% more"), and discount structures. One tap sends it via n8n's WhatsApp automation.

Cash Flow Prophet

Predicts next 7 days. Alerts before shortfalls hit.

Every merchant has a rhythm. Ramesh does ₹15,000 on Saturdays, ₹8,000 on Tuesdays, dips on Wednesdays. Netrā builds a daily revenue model from 90 days of transactions, capturing day-of-week rhythms, payday spending cycles (1st/15th), and seasonal baselines. If a shortfall looms, it recommends action: "Thursday looks ₹4,000 short. Run a flash offer Tuesday to pull demand forward." For Paytm, this doubles as a stealth underwriting signal for merchant credit.

Growth Missions

Weekly micro-tasks learned from peer merchants.

Netrā groups merchants into cohorts by location tier, category, and revenue. Within each cohort, it tracks which merchants GREW in the last 90 days and what preceded that growth. Netrā assigns a weekly mission: "This week, add 3 items from the trending list. Stores like yours that did this saw 11% higher weekly revenue." Outcome feedback loops back into Cognee to continuously strengthen peer signals. This is the flywheel. This is the moat.

Why This Matters for Paytm

Subscription Stickiness

At ₹100–300/month, Soundbox is kept only while useful. Netrā elevates it from a passive payment chime into a vital daily intelligence asset. Merchants will never cancel, protecting Paytm's ₹1,500+ Cr recurring subscription revenue.

Compounding Data Moat

Every active store enriches local demand heatmaps and cohort mission quality. No competitor (PhonePe, Google Pay, BharatPe) can duplicate this without Paytm's vast network of 4.9 crore merchants. A network moat that deepens daily.

Merchant Lending Funnel

Cash Flow Prophet acts as real-time predictive underwriting. Stores with stable liquidity and growing cohort ranks become pre-qualified borrowers, feeding Paytm's highest-margin lending division at near-zero customer acquisition cost.
