## GLOBAL DESIGN LANGUAGE

This section governs the ENTIRE deck. Every slide follows these rules unless a specific slide overrides them.

**Color Palette:**
- Wine (#722F37) — headings, emphasis blocks, title/closing slide backgrounds
- Creme White (#FFF8F0) — primary slide backgrounds, text on wine backgrounds
- Soft Gold (#C9A96E) — stat numbers, accent lines on diagrams, icon highlights
- Charcoal (#2D2D2D) — body text, diagram labels
- Muted Wine (#8B4049) — secondary text, captions, supporting info
- Soft Sand (#F5EDE3) — card backgrounds on creme slides for subtle depth

**Typography:**
- Slide titles: Poppins Bold or Montserrat Bold, 28-32pt, wine colored on creme backgrounds, creme on wine backgrounds
- Body text: Inter or DM Sans, 14-16pt, charcoal. Generous line spacing (1.4x to 1.6x)
- Stats and big numbers: Poppins Bold, 48-64pt, gold or wine. These are visual anchors, not just text.
- Captions and labels: Inter Light, 10-12pt, muted wine
- Feature names: Poppins SemiBold, 18-20pt, wine

**Design Taste — What Makes This Not Bland:**
- Use SOFT SAND (#F5EDE3) cards with rounded corners (12-16px radius) as containers for content blocks. These create visual hierarchy without borders or lines. Cards float on the creme background with subtle box shadows (2-4px, low opacity).
- Diagrams and flowcharts should use rounded rectangles with wine fills and creme text for primary nodes, sand fills with charcoal text for secondary nodes, and thin gold connection lines (2px).
- Icons should be simple line icons (Lucide style) in wine color, placed inside soft gold circles (40-48px) as visual anchors for each section.
- Use subtle grain/noise texture on the wine background slides (title and closing). Not flat color. A faint paper-like grain that adds warmth and physicality.
- Use generous whitespace as a design element. Empty space is not wasted space. It gives each element room to land. Target 30-40% whitespace per slide.
- NO clip art. NO stock photos. NO gradients (except very subtle ones on the wine backgrounds). NO decorative bars or accent stripes. NO underlines below titles. The design language is warm minimalism with material texture.
- Every flowchart and diagram should feel hand-crafted, not auto-generated. Rounded corners, consistent spacing between nodes, clear directional arrows (wine or gold colored, with arrowheads).
- The overall aesthetic: premium, warm, confident. Think a well-designed pitch deck from a YC-backed startup, not a college project. It should look like something Paytm's own design team would present internally.

**Slide Layout Rules:**
- All slides have 0.6" margins on all sides minimum
- Title at the top, left-aligned or centered depending on slide type
- Content occupies the middle 70% of the slide vertically. Top and bottom breathe.
- No more than 3 distinct content blocks per slide (e.g., 3 cards, or 1 diagram + 1 text block + 1 stat)
- Every element earns its place. If removing it does not weaken the slide, it should not be there.

### SLIDE 4 — WHAT NETRĀ DOES (The Intelligence Layer)

**Purpose:** Now that the judge understands the INSIGHT (network intelligence), this slide shows WHAT the system actually does with it. This is the first of two product deep-dive slides. This one covers the INTELLIGENCE side: Trade Radar and Price Pulse — the two features that analyze the market around the merchant. The next slide covers the ACTION side.

**Title:** "The Intelligence Layer — Reading the Market" — Poppins Bold, 26pt, Wine, top-left

**Layout:** Two large feature blocks, stacked vertically, each taking ~45% of the content area. Each block is a Soft Sand card with rounded corners.

**Feature Block 1 — Trade Radar:**

Left side of the card: Icon (radar/signal waves in wine, inside a gold circle, 48px).
Right side of the card: Content.

Name: "Trade Radar" (Poppins SemiBold, 20pt, Wine)
One-liner: "See what nearby consumers are buying that you do not stock." (Inter Medium, 14pt, Charcoal)

How it works (Inter Regular, 13pt, Charcoal, spaced out as a flowing paragraph, NOT bullet points):
"Netrā clusters every merchant into a hyperlocal micro-market — roughly a 1 to 2 km radius zone aligned with a real neighborhood (Lajpat Nagar, not 'Delhi'). Within that zone, it aggregates transaction volumes by product category across all merchants. Beverages up 40% this week. Snacks steady. Personal care down 10%. Then it compares this market-level demand heatmap against YOUR transaction categories. If iced beverages are surging in Lajpat Nagar and Ramesh has zero transactions in that category, that is a gap. That gap becomes a Trade Radar alert: 'Iced tea is up 40% in your pin code. You stock none. Top 3 items to try based on what similar stores sell: [X, Y, Z].' The ML underneath is collaborative filtering on a merchant-category matrix, localized by micro-market. Lightweight. Interpretable. Runs as a daily batch job on Paytm's existing transaction data."

**Feature Block 2 — Price Pulse:**

Same card layout as above. Icon: pulse/heartbeat line in wine inside gold circle.

Name: "Price Pulse" (Poppins SemiBold, 20pt, Wine)
One-liner: "Know where your pricing stands in the local market." (Inter Medium, 14pt, Charcoal)

How it works (same paragraph style):
"Netrā computes the average transaction value per product category per micro-market. Across all kiranas in Lajpat Nagar, the median snack transaction is Rs 35. Ramesh's average is Rs 40 — he is pricing 14% above the median. Netrā tracks this gap over time: is it widening? Are nearby merchants lowering prices? Is his volume dropping as a result? When it detects a risk, it does not just say 'you are expensive' — that is demoralizing and useless. It frames it as an opportunity: 'Your snack pricing is 14% above the area average and your volume dipped 8% this week. A weekend combo — namkeen + cold drink at Rs 30 — could pull price-sensitive customers back without a permanent price cut.' No merchant ever sees another merchant's prices. Only aggregate market averages. Privacy is absolute. The model is statistical outlier detection on average-transaction-value distributions, tracked over a rolling 30-day window."

**Design notes:** These two cards should feel like pages from a premium product spec. The icon-circle on the left anchors the eye. The content on the right tells the full story: what it does, how it does it, what the merchant sees, and what is under the hood. The judge reads this and thinks "these guys know exactly how to build this." Keep the text generous in line spacing — it is dense content but should not feel cramped.

How it works (Inter Regular, 12pt, Charcoal):
"Netrā maintains a master calendar of Indian festivals — not just Diwali and Holi, but Chhath Puja, Onam, Pongal, Baisakhi, local melas, exam seasons, wedding season, monsoon shifts. For each merchant, it pulls their sales data from the same festival last year: what spiked, what sold out, what sat on the shelf. Then Sarvam's LLM generates a complete, ready-to-send offer package: a promotional WhatsApp message in the merchant's language, a recommended inventory prep list ('you ran out of X last time — order 20% more'), and a suggested discount structure with projected margin impact. The merchant sees one card in their Paytm app. One tap sends the offer to their repeat customer base via n8n's WhatsApp automation. Minimum effort, maximum preparation."

**Feature Block 2 — Cash Flow Prophet:**
Icon: Trending line with alert dot, wine in gold circle.
Name: "Cash Flow Prophet" (Poppins SemiBold, 18pt, Wine)
One-liner: "Predicts next 7 days of cash flow. Alerts before shortfalls hit." (Inter Medium, 13pt, Charcoal)

How it works:
"Every merchant has a rhythm. Ramesh does Rs 15,000 on Saturdays, Rs 8,000 on Tuesdays, dips on Wednesdays. Netrā builds a per-merchant daily revenue model from 90 days of transaction data, capturing day-of-week effects, payday cycles (spending spikes on the 1st and 15th), and seasonal baselines. It adjusts for detected anomalies — festival bumps, weather disruptions, post-offer demand dips. If the 7-day forecast shows a shortfall, Netrā does not just warn: it suggests a specific move. 'Thursday looks Rs 4,000 short. Run a flash offer Tuesday to pull demand forward.' The prediction engine is Prophet-style time-series forecasting, per merchant, lightweight, runs on daily aggregated totals. For Paytm, this feature doubles as a stealth lending signal: merchants with stable, growing cash flows are pre-qualified borrowers."

**Feature Block 3 — Growth Missions:**
Icon: Target/bullseye, wine in gold circle.
Name: "Growth Missions" (Poppins SemiBold, 18pt, Wine)
One-liner: "Weekly micro-tasks from the network. What worked for merchants like you." (Inter Medium, 13pt, Charcoal)

How it works:
"Netrā groups merchants into cohorts — same location tier, business category, revenue range, transaction volume. Within each cohort, it tracks which merchants GREW in the last 90 days and what specific behaviors preceded that growth. Did they add new categories? Run weekend offers? Extend hours? For a merchant who has not yet tried a growth-correlated behavior, Netrā assigns a 'mission': 'This week, add 3 items from the trending-nearby list. Stores like yours that did this saw 11% higher weekly revenue.' After the mission period, Netrā checks whether the merchant acted and what happened. The outcome feeds back into the model: if it worked, the signal strengthens for the cohort. If not, the system adjusts. This is the feature that makes Netrā a NETWORK product. Every merchant's experience makes every other merchant's recommendations smarter. This is the flywheel. This is the moat."

**Design notes:** Three cards, tighter than Slide 4 but still breathing. The key is that each card tells a complete story: what the merchant sees, what happens under the hood, and why it matters. A judge reading just the italic examples should get the gist. A judge reading the full text should feel convinced this team can build it.

## WHAT A JUDGE WILL ASK (for the in-person day)

Q: "How is this different from the existing Paytm AI Soundbox?"
A: The existing Soundbox reads payment confirmations and basic summaries. Netrā adds PROACTIVE growth intelligence: hyperlocal demand signals, competitive pricing context, festival preparation, cash flow forecasting, and network-learned growth missions. The difference between a receipt printer and a business advisor.

Q: "What about merchant data privacy?"
A: All network intelligence is aggregated and anonymized. No merchant sees another merchant's data. The system operates on PATTERNS across thousands of merchants, not individual records. Like Google Maps traffic: your phone contributes data, no one sees your specific location.

Q: "Can this scale to 4.9 crore merchants?"
A: The data already exists — Paytm processes billions of transactions. The aggregation pipeline is a daily batch job. Sarvam's models run inference efficiently. n8n handles workflow distribution. The hardest part (collecting the data) is done. We are just unlocking it.

Q: "Why Growth Missions and not just a dashboard?"
A: Dashboards are passive — they wait for the merchant to look. 80% of small merchants will never open a dashboard regularly. Missions are PROACTIVE, specific, and small. "Add 3 trending items" is actionable. "Here is your analytics page" is not. And missions create the flywheel: each merchant's outcome makes the next merchant's mission smarter.

Q: "Why this track over Track 2 or 3?"
A: Track 1 is closest to Paytm's actual strategic priority. Paytm's revenue comes from merchants. A product that makes merchants grow and stay is a product that grows Paytm's topline and reduces churn. Also the least crowded track — more space to stand out.
